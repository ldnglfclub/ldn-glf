✦ The Story

Started at twelve.
Refused to fit in.

I'm Dylan Heer. I started playing golf at twelve and fell in love with the game the first time a putt dropped exactly the way I'd seen it. The game itself is beautiful. The world around it isn't always.

The clothes weren't made for me. The clubs felt like museums. None of it spoke my language. So I'm building what I wish existed — a London golf club that looks how my generation actually wants it to look, costs what we can actually afford, and treats young players like the future, not the entry fee.

This is day one. Get on the list and you're not a customer — you're a founder.

— Dylan, London 2026
