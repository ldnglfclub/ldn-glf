✦ Our Mission ✦

Golf has been locked away
from young people
for far too long.

Too expensive. Too snobby. Too gatekept by dress codes, country clubs, and people who forgot what it's like to be young and just want to play.

We're a movement of young Londoners changing that. Not by talking about it. By building the community we wish existed when we started — and opening the door for every kid behind us.

01

Access

Every young person should know there's a place they can play, what it costs, and that they'll be welcome there. We map it all out, free.

02

Community

Young golfers in London shouldn't be playing alone. We're building the network that turns this game from a solo sport into a shared journey.

03

Growth

The course teaches focus, honesty, resilience, patience. We help young people get better at the game — and at being people.

✦ The Founder

Started at twelve.
Built it for us.

I'm Dylan. I started playing golf at twelve and fell in love the first time a putt dropped exactly the way I'd seen it. The game is beautiful. The world around it isn't always.

I've stood on tee boxes where I felt watched for not having the right gear. I've walked into clubs where the look on the starter's face told me everything. I've seen mates put their clubs down because someone made them feel small. None of that should happen.

So I'm building this — with the help of an adult team handling the boring bits — for every young person in London who wants in. The 100 founding members aren't customers. They're co-founders. You help shape what this becomes.

— Dylan, London 2026
