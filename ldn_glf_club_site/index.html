Our Manifesto

Golf was never meant to belong to one type of person.

We’re building a club for the kids who play at the range after school, who tee off at Topgolf with their mates, who care about the cut of a hoodie as much as the line of a putt. The dress code is taste. The handicap is irrelevant.

— A club without a clubhouse

From the Founder

I started playing golf at twelve. I fell in love with the game, but never with the world around it. The clothes weren’t made for me. The clubs weren’t made for me. None of it felt like mine. So I built something that is — for me, for my mates, and for every kid who’s ever stood on a tee box and thought there had to be a better way to do this.

— The Founder
