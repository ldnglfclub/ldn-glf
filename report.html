✦ Safeguarding

Report a concern.

If something happened — at a course, on this site, in a chat, anywhere in the LDN GLF Club world — and it doesn't feel right, tell us. We take every report seriously and we'll never make you feel small for raising it.

⚠ If you're in danger right now

Don't wait — get help now.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999. These free, confidential helplines are open right now if you need to talk to someone trained:

24/7 · Free · Under 19 0808 800 5000 NSPCC

What happens after you hit send

  1. Your report goes only to the LDN GLF Club safeguarding lead. No one else sees it.
  2. We respond within 24 hours — sooner if you've said it's urgent.
  3. If a crime may have been committed, or a child is at risk of harm, we'll involve the police or the local council's children's services. We'll tell you when we do.
  4. You can withdraw your report at any time — email safeguarding@ldnglfclub.com.
