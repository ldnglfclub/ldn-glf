Privacy Policy

Last updated · 27 May 2026 · GDPR / UK Data Protection Act 2018

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are.

What we collect

  • Email address — when you sign up to the mailing list.
  • Name — optionally, if you choose to provide it.
  • Basic analytics — page views, browser type, country (via Vercel's built-in tools). No tracking pixels, no third-party advertising trackers.

What we don't collect

  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not use Facebook Pixel, Google Ads tags, or any retargeting tracker.
  • We do not collect financial information on this site (any future purchases will go through Stripe, who handle their own data).

What we use it for

  • Sending you updates about drops, the app, and club news (when you sign up to the list).
  • Understanding which pages people read so we can improve the site.
  • Nothing else. No selling. No sharing. No third-party marketing.

Your rights under UK GDPR

  • Access — you can ask what data we hold on you, and we'll tell you.
  • Deletion — you can ask us to delete your data and we'll do it within 30 days.
  • Correction — you can ask us to correct anything wrong.
  • Withdrawal — you can unsubscribe from the mailing list at any time via the link in every email.

If you are under 16

We apply the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 without verified parental consent. If you are 13–16, we encourage you to tell a parent or guardian you are signing up.

Where data is stored

Our mailing list is hosted by Formspree (US-based, GDPR compliant). The site is hosted by Vercel (US-based, GDPR compliant). Anonymous analytics may be processed by Vercel.

Questions?

Email us via the contact page. We respond within 48 hours.

Cookie Policy

Last updated · 27 May 2026

This site uses minimal cookies. We don't use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or third-party tracking cookies.

What we use

  • Strictly necessary cookies — used by our hosting provider Vercel to keep the site secure and fast. Cannot be disabled if you want to use the site.
  • Anonymous analytics — Vercel's built-in analytics counts visits without identifying individuals. No cookies are stored on your device for this.

What we don't use

  • Google Analytics
  • Facebook Pixel
  • Advertising or remarketing cookies
  • Cookies that profile your behaviour across other websites

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies via your browser settings. Doing so will not affect your ability to use this site.

Returns & Refunds Policy

We don't currently sell anything on this site. When we do, this is the policy you can expect:

Returns

  • 14-day return window from receipt of order, in line with the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags attached.
  • Custom or numbered items (e.g. limited drops with personalisation) may be non-returnable — this will be flagged at checkout.

Refunds

  • Refunded to the original payment method within 14 days of us receiving the returned item.
  • Original shipping is refunded if you're returning a faulty item; not refunded if you've simply changed your mind.

Faulty items

If something arrives broken, miswoven, miscoloured or otherwise not as described — let us know within 30 days and we'll replace it or refund it in full, including return shipping.

How to start a return

Email us via the contact page with your order number and reason. We respond within 48 hours.
