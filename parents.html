For Parents

A safer place
to love golf.

If your kid wants to join us, you should know exactly who we are, what we stand for, and how we'll look after them. No spin. Just the truth.

Who we are.

The LDN GLF Club was founded in 2026 by Dylan, a London golfer who started playing at twelve. He loved the game but didn't see himself in the world around it — the clothes, the clubs, the gatekeeping. So he built something for kids like him.

Dylan is supported by a small adult team handling the boring-but-essential bits: business operations, safeguarding, financial admin, and compliance. The brand belongs to Dylan. The responsibility belongs to all of us.

We're a UK-registered business. All transactions, communications, and member data are handled by responsible adults under UK law.

Our promises to you.

Six commitments we make to every parent whose kid joins the club.

01

No personal data sold. Ever.

We don't sell, rent, or share your child's email, name, or any personal data with third parties for marketing. Full stop. (See our Privacy Policy.)

02

Age-appropriate everything.

Content, language, products, partner venues — all reviewed against an under-18 lens. We will never promote alcohol, gambling, or adult content to members.

03

Moderated community.

If the club introduces a group chat or social feed, an adult on the team will be present, behaviour will be monitored, and our Code (no racism, no bullying, no harassment) will be enforced.

04

Easy off-ramp.

One email to us deletes the entire account, removes the member from any chats, and erases personal data within 30 days. No friction, no questions, no upsells.

05

Transparent finances.

Membership is free. If we ever introduce paid tiers or product purchases, prices will be clear and parental consent will be required for any under-16 transaction.

06

A real human to talk to.

If something goes wrong — a comment in a chat, a concern about an interaction, anything — you can email us and get a response from a real adult within 48 hours.

Why golf is good for young people.

It's not just our opinion. Sport England, the R&A, and decades of research agree: golf is one of the most under-rated youth sports.

Mental health

Four hours outdoors, walking, focused on something that isn't a screen. Golf is one of the few sports young people can do at their own pace, on their own terms, with their own thoughts.

Honesty & integrity

Golf is the only sport where you call your own penalties. Kids who play golf learn to be honest even when nobody's watching — a real-world skill that matters far beyond the course.

Resilience

You can hit a perfect shot, then a terrible one, then have to focus on the next. Learning to handle the swing between confidence and frustration is one of golf's quiet gifts.

Intergenerational

Golf is one of the only sports a 12-year-old can play with a 60-year-old, equally. The relationships built on a course often last decades.

Focus & patience

In a world built for instant gratification, golf demands the opposite: hours of practice, slow improvement, no shortcuts. That patience compounds.

Outdoors, low-cost, year-round

Modern golf doesn't require a country club membership. Driving ranges, pitch-and-putt, par 3s — there are dozens of affordable, welcoming places listed in our Course Atlas.

Safeguarding our way.

1. Adult-led operations. Although Dylan is the founder and creative lead, all business-critical functions — accounts, payments, contracts, partnerships, safeguarding response — are handled by named adults on the team.

2. Verified age on signup. Members under 13 require parental email confirmation before their account is activated. We follow the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code).

3. No private DMs between adults and minors. If we introduce in-app messaging, communication between the team and under-18 members happens in moderated channels visible to multiple adults.

4. Clear reporting routes. Every page on the site has a contact link. Any member or parent can flag concerns directly to the safeguarding lead.

5. Annual training. All adults handling member data or running events complete safeguarding training annually.

6. Partner vetting. Any venue we partner with for events or discounts is checked for its own youth policies. We won't partner with venues that exclude or harass young players.

Got a question?

Anything — about Dylan, about safeguarding, about a specific event, about what your kid's joining. Real reply within 48 hours.
