✦ Course 01 — South West London

Richmond Park —
Prince's Course

Opened 1923 · Designed by Fred Hawtree · Redesigned 2013

68
Par
5,487
Yards (W)
66.9
Course Rating

The shorter, more forgiving of Richmond Park's two courses — perfect for juniors, beginners and members getting comp-ready. Sits on the higher ground of the park. Big skies, mature oaks, the occasional deer wandering across a fairway. Don't let "Par 68" fool you: tight tree-lined corridors and small greens punish a sloppy round. The 2013 redesign added seven new greens and tightened the routing — this is a proper test in a smaller package.

✦ Course 02 — South West London

Richmond Park —
Duke's Course

Opened 1925 · Designed by Fred Hawtree · The longer test

71
Par
6,359
Yards (W)
71.1
Course Rating

The grown-up sibling. Beverley Brook — the park's ancient stream — winds through holes 3–6 on the front and re-enters play on 12–14 on the back. Slope rating of 131 off the whites tells you everything: you need to think your way round. Longer par-4s, more carries, less margin. If Prince's is where you learn the game, Duke's is where you prove you've got it.

✦ Course 03 — North London / Barnet

The Shire London —
Ballesteros Masters

Opened 2007 · Designed by Seve Ballesteros — his only UK course

72
Par
7,028
Yards
6/6/6
3s / 4s / 5s

Seve's masterpiece on the edge of London. Six par-3s, six par-4s, six par-5s — no two consecutive holes the same par. Twelve holes feature water. The signature par-3 8th plays over a lake. The closing 18th green is wrapped by an S-shaped lake — Seve literally signed his initial on the land. Built with soil excavated from Wembley, the Emirates and Wimbledon. A proper bucket-list round, ten miles from central London.
